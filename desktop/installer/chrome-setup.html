Add WriteMind to Google Chrome

Four quick steps — then WriteMind checks your writing in Gmail, WhatsApp Web, LinkedIn and every text box.

  1. Open Google Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
    Paste it into the address bar and press Enter.
  2. Turn on Developer mode with the switch in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
    the “Chrome extension” folder where WriteMind is installed
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome’s toolbar and pin WriteMind so it’s always one click away.

Your text stays private: WriteMind checks it on your computer and never changes it unless you click a suggestion.
